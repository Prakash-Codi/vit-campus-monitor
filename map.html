1. Cartographic Layer Guide & Sourced Nodes

The map features 7 toggleable domain layers available in the top-right layer controller:

  • ⚡ Electricity Load: Proportional circles reflecting estimated instantaneous kW and powerhouse kVA capacity. Scales dynamically with the 2019–2021 month timeline.
  • 🌡️ Microclimate / Temperature: Highlights thermal differences across the campus: -3°C vegetative shade canopy around academic avenues vs hot tarmac zones (SMEC heavy bays, outdoor sports complex).
  • 💨 CO₂ Point Emitters & Sinks: Pinpoints point sources of combustion (Automotive & IC Engine labs at SMEC, SCHEME Chemical Process Lab, 31 DG exhaust stacks) juxtaposed against vegetative sinks (18.69-acre canopy absorbing 1,869 t CO₂/yr).
  • 🏢 Hostels (32 Blocks): Men's Hostels (South & North high-rises) and Ladies' Hostels, documenting resident density, toilet infrastructure, and sanitary waste handling.
  • 🔬 Labs & Academic Blocks: Classrooms and research facilities (TT, PRP, SJT, Gandhi Block, CDMM, CBMR).
  • 🔌 Transformers & Substations: 33/11 kV main substation and 12 powerhouse stations.
  • 🌱 Renewables & District Cooling: Rooftop solar PV installations (2.113 MWp / 2,813 kWp), 5,500 TR district chiller plants, biogas digestor (300 m³), and solar hot water arrays.

2. Visual Demarcation: Measured vs Modelled

In rigorous compliance with academic research standards, measured data points are visually distinguished from modelled approximations:

  • Solid Border [Measured]: Data originating directly from TANGEDCO utility revenue meters, 33 kV incoming CT/PT logs, or certified audit testing.
  • Dashed Border [Modelled Estimate]: Building and lab-level electrical allocations derived mathematically from connected transformer ratings, known machine wattage, floor areas, and student population densities. Full formulas are catalogued on the Methods page.

Geospatial Data Notes
  1. Geocoding Sources: Building coordinates originate from OpenStreetMap (OSM) Katpadi node queries and ground verification. Outlying or uncatalogued facilities are placed using official campus estate layouts and marked as "approx. location".
  2. Dynamic Timeline Scaling: When scrubbing the month slider, the Electricity layer radii modulate proportionally to total campus billing kWh recorded in that respective month (from 4.69M units peak in Apr 2019 down to 3.67L units in Apr 2020).
