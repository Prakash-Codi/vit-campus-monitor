2. Mathematical Formulations & Modelling Logic

Because room-level and lab-level IoT energy sub-meters do not exist campus-wide, local hotspot intensities are transparently calculated using the following engineering approximations:

A. Electrical Load Estimation (kW)

For unmetered academic towers, peak electrical load \(P_{\text{est}}\) is estimated from connected transformer capacity and operational load factors:

P_est (kW) = S_tr (kVA) × cos(φ) × LF_diversity

Where \(S_{\text{tr}}\) is the dedicated transformer capacity (e.g. 1,600 kVA for Tech Tower, 2,000 kVA for Pearl Research Park), \(\cos(\phi) = 0.99\) (maintained via thyristor-switched APFC capacitor banks as per Green Audit p. 47), and \(LF_{\text{diversity}}\) is the measured diversity factor (0.50 to 0.65).

B. Carbon Dioxide Sequestration (Forest & Open Land)

Campus absorption figures follow empirical forestry formulas adopted in Environmental Audit 2024 (p. 20):

  • Canopy Trees: 18.69 acres (75,649 m²) @ 100 t CO₂/acre/year = 1,869.00 t/year
  • Open Porous Land: 6,31,928 m² @ 1.0 × 10⁻⁶ mol CO₂/(m²·s) = 876.00 t/year
  • Lawns & Shrubs: 35.60 hectares @ 1.95 t CO₂/ha/year = 69.44 t/year
  • Total Annual Natural Sequestration: 2,814.44 t CO₂/year

C. Scope 1 Combustion & Scope 2 Grid Emissions

Calculated using standard IPCC emission factors applied by Eco Services India:

E_gross = (kWh_grid × 0.813) + (L_diesel × 0.00333) + (L_petrol × 0.00166) + (L_LPG × 0.00152)
E_net = E_gross − Absorption_campus

Known Data Quirks Handled

1. The 103.66% RES Anomaly in 2021
The 2020 Energy Audit table (p. 6) lists a Renewable Energy Share of 103.659% for the year 2021. This occurred because the audit was compiled mid-year 2021 during COVID-19 remote instruction. The denominator only contained 6 months of restricted campus load (27.42 lakh units), while contracted wind wheeling credits (17.32 lakh units) and solar generation (11.10 lakh units) exceeded that partial total. In this dashboard, 2021 is clearly flagged as a part-year artifact.
2. April 2019 Bill Discrepancy
Energy Audit text (p. 4) casually writes "40 Lakh units, the highest during 2019", whereas the official billing ledger table directly below it confirms 46,88,760 HT units and ₹3,44,13,114. The official billing log is retained as the authoritative data source.
3. Ambient CO₂ Telemetry
Standard urban meteorological weather stations and open APIs report PM2.5, NO₂, SO₂, and CO, but do not operate ambient CO₂ ppm sensors. City-wide CO₂ concentration is documented as global background (~421 ppm), while campus emission hotspots are modelled from verified equipment and fuel ledgers.
