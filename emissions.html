1. Gross Emissions Breakdown by Source (2024)

Grid-supplied electricity dominates the university's operational emissions footprint. While fuel combustion in 31 backup diesel generators (2,30,000 L/yr), student shuttles (2,83,057 L/yr), and kitchen LPG (16.61 lakh L/yr) contributes 4,246 tonnes CO₂e, purchased electricity represents 23,968 tonnes CO₂e.

3. Point-Source Emitters & Laboratory Verification

Beyond utility electricity, specific engineering disciplines host localized emission sources identified during field audits:

  • Automotive & IC Engine Labs (SMEC): Houses multi-cylinder test rigs and dynamometers burning diesel and petrol fuel blends for experimental combustion analysis. Exhaust is routed through dedicated scrubbers and elevated flues.
  • Chemical & Process Engineering Labs (SCHEME / SMV): Operates batch reaction kettles, solvent recovery columns, and thermal units. Organic chemical wastes are safely sequestered in cement chambers and handed to TNPCB-authorized recyclers.
  • Emergency DG Sets (31 units, 16.9 MVA): Diesel backup capacity operated during grid failures and monsoon emergencies. Fitted with acoustic enclosures and stack heights up to 24 meters in compliance with CPCB air pollution guidelines.
