Hostel Accommodation Architecture

The residential infrastructure houses 21,936 students across 32 individual blocks (21 for male students, 11 for female students), with an average occupancy of ~686 students per block. Older low-rise blocks (A through F) are characterized by open internal courtyards and natural ventilation. Later phases (L, M, N, P, Q, R, and S) represent high-rise towers up to 16 storeys equipped with variable-frequency ACVVF elevators and central district cooling connections.

Domestic hot water demand in hostels has been systematically decoupled from electrical resistance heating. A total of 47,000 Litres Per Day (LPD) of rooftop solar flat-plate collectors work in tandem with 16,800 Litres Per Hour of centralized heat pump water heaters (COP 4.0), delivering 55°C water while consuming only 25% of the electricity required by immersion boilers.
